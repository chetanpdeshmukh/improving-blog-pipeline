**3. Checkpoint 5A/5B - No Question-Format H2s**

Location: All four H2 headings.

Problem: None of the H2s are phrased as questions. This reduces snippet eligibility and search alignment for how/what/why queries.

Fix direction: Convert one or two H2s to question format. "Which Metrics Actually Diagnose Inference Latency?" or "When Does GPU Monitoring Fail as Your Primary Signal?" are natural fits for the content already present.

**4. Checkpoint 6A - Two Technical Figures Need Verification**

Location 1: "two DGX boxes at 8 KW apiece fill a standard 20 KW rack"

Problem: DGX H100 system power draw is approximately 10.2 kW, not 8 kW. At 10 kW each, two units filling a 20 kW rack is accurate; at 8 kW each, the math leaves 4 kW of rack capacity unexplained. The figure as written is internally inconsistent.

Fix direction: Verify the power draw figure with Aman Jain or against the DGX H100 spec sheet. If 10 kW, update to "approximately 10 KW apiece."

Location 2: "Llama 3.1 8B quantized measured 23.2 GB on InfraCloud's live deployment against a 24 GB ceiling"

Problem: Quantized 8B models in INT8 are typically ~8 GB in weights; in BF16 they run ~16 GB. 23.2 GB as a "quantized" figure is higher than base BF16 weights, suggesting the measurement includes KV cache allocation, runtime overhead, or that the quantization level is light (GPTQ or similar with minimal reduction). The label "quantized" against a 24 GB RTX 4090 ceiling at 23.2 GB implies near-full VRAM saturation, which is a meaningful operational fact - but the quantization framing may confuse readers who expect quantized to mean smaller.

Fix direction: Confirm with Aman Jain what quantization format was used and whether 23.2 GB reflects total VRAM allocation (weights + KV cache + runtime) or weights only. Add a parenthetical clarifying the measurement scope if it includes runtime overhead.

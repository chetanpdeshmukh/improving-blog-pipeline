**2. [2A] Source transcript not verifiable in this review**

No transcript was provided alongside the draft. The editorial note at the bottom indicates changes were made to match "what actually happened," which suggests transcript alignment was considered. Flag for the pipeline log -- this WARN is procedural, not a content quality concern.

**3. [5A] No H2s in question format**

Current H2s ("The problem started with a merge," "The actual fix") are narrative and descriptive but do not match the question patterns users search for.
Direction: Convert at least two H2s. Examples: "Why does Azure AD Connect fail when merging on-premises forests?" or "How do you fix proxyAddresses sync errors after an acquisition?"

**4. [5B] No snippet-ready Q&A sections**

Follows directly from 5A. Question-format H2s with a direct 2-3 sentence answer underneath would make this eligible for featured snippet placement.
Direction: Address alongside 5A fix above.

**5. [5E] No structured element for AI citation**

The article has quotable lines and Improving attribution, but no table, numbered checklist, or decision framework. For AI citation readiness, a "Pre-migration schema audit checklist" or a numbered step sequence for the fix would provide the structured anchor.
Direction: The PowerShell fix description (scan, generate, write back) could be rendered as a three-step numbered list without losing tone.

**6. [6A] Terminology inconsistency and one technical imprecision**

- The intro uses "Entra ID" but every subsequent reference uses "Azure AD" and "Azure AD Connect." Microsoft renamed Azure AD to Microsoft Entra ID and Azure AD Connect to Microsoft Entra Connect in 2023. The article should pick one and use it consistently throughout -- current naming preferred.
- "Split the sync into two connector spaces" is imprecise. A connector space is a staging buffer that belongs to a connector; you cannot split into two connector spaces without creating two connectors. The fix described sounds like two separate connectors with different OU scoping, not a connector-space split.
Direction: Verify with the author whether they created two connectors or used scoping filters on one connector. Update the sentence accordingly.

**7. [4A] Word count approximately 500 -- well under the 776 floor (non-blocking)**

This is logged for visibility only and does not affect the Verdict. The article reads short because the story is tight, not because it is padded or incomplete. If expansion is warranted, "The actual fix" and "What we'd do differently" are the two sections with room to go deeper without adding filler: the PowerShell script logic and the schema audit approach could each carry another paragraph of specific detail.

### FAIL Items (Fix Before Publishing)

**1. Checkpoint 3B — Banned word: "stakeholders" (×6 instances)**

Every instance:
- "transcript synthesis after a stakeholder call"
- "Sprint reviews still ended with stakeholders saying 'that's not what we meant.'"
- "Early-stage discovery conversations, stakeholder interviews, and requirement workshops"
- "Every stakeholder transcript goes through it."
- "stakeholders said 'wow' about contributions from people who had just joined"
- "locked scope and aligned stakeholders"

Problem: "stakeholders" is explicitly listed as a banned vague noun. The rule is absolute — every instance is a FAIL.

Fix direction: Replace with the specific people meant in each instance. "A call with the product owner and business sponsor" rather than "a stakeholder call." "The people funding the sprint" rather than "aligned stakeholders." "The clients at sprint review" rather than "stakeholders said 'wow'." Each replacement should reflect who Clauddio's examples actually involved — the substitution will also strengthen the prose.

**2. Checkpoint 4D — Near-verbatim restatement across Section 3 and Section 7**

Section 7, paragraph 2: "The story agent produces well-organized output from whatever the conversation contained. When facilitation fails to surface the real need, the agent surfaces the same gap with better formatting."
